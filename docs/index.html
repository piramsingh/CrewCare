Members keep their health. Their union gets what it needs to act.

A text before every shift about the air at their station. Health answers that never leave the phone. One report that reaches their union rep.

23 seconds, with sound. Every screen is the real product and every figure is one the service returns.

A transit worker shouldn't have to hand over their medical history to report a hazard.

No public air quality feed exists for the subway, and no simple way to flag a bad platform. CrewCares does both, and keeps a hard line between them.

How it works

No app to install. All four steps happen in a text thread.

01 — Before the shift

A text 90 minutes before they report.

Where they're working today, and what the air is doing there. The instruction, not the evidence.

03 — The one thing that leaves

A complaint goes to their union rep. Nothing else does.

No category picker. Just the sentence only they can write. The station and date come along on their own.
