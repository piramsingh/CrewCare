23 seconds, with sound. Every screen is the real product and every figure is one the service returns.

A transit worker shouldn't have to hand over their medical history to report a hazard.

No public air quality feed exists for the subway, and no simple way to flag a bad platform. CrewCare does both, and keeps a hard line between them.

How it works

No app to install. All four steps happen in a text thread.

What's real and what isn't

Built at a hackathon in a weekend, and labelled this way on every screen, not just here.

Real
The privacy architecture and the physical models. Live outdoor data from Open-Meteo, 496 stations from NYS Open Data, the whole flow running end to end.
Modelled, not measured
Every platform figure, estimated from live street-level data because no per-station feed exists. A prior for where to look first, never a clearance that a station is safe.
Simulated
The 449 complaints in the repository are a synthetic corpus, not real submissions from anyone.
Not built
Authentication, a live messaging integration, and the Apple Health bridge. The demo simulates all three.

Every formula, constant and stated limitation is written up in METHODOLOGY.md.

The boundary is the product, not a setting.

The code is public, so you don't have to take that on trust.
